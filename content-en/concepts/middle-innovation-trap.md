# Middle Innovation Trap

**Definition**: a situation in which a country attains high **implementation capability**
(strong at manufacturing, process improvement) but fails to develop **concept design
capability** (the ability to ask big questions on its own and design market-leading
concepts/products) → growth stalls again even after escaping the [[middle-income-trap]].

## In the course

- The focus of Essay Topic Area 2 ([[essays-instructions]]): escaping the trap requires a
  national-level, long-term effort coordinated across firms, government, and institutions.
- Proposed way out: [[creative-accumulation]] — accumulating trial-and-error experience,
  mission-oriented policy; case study: South Korea's industrial trajectory as a lesson for
  Vietnam.
