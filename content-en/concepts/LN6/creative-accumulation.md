# Creative Accumulation

**Definition**: the process of building the capabilities needed for sustainable technological
development and rising to technological leadership, beyond pure catch-up growth. It requires:
asking bold questions, persistently accumulating trial-and-error experience, and scaling up from
ideas to viable outcomes.

## Within the Course

- Presented as an **escape route from the [[middle-innovation-trap]]**
  ([[essays-instructions]], Topic Area 2).
- Theoretical foundation: innovation theory + evolutionary economics + industrial policy.
- Policy prescriptions: coordinated, mission-oriented, long-run strategies — applicable at every
  stage of development.
- Central case study: **South Korea's** industry & technology trajectory — is it a model for
  Vietnam?
