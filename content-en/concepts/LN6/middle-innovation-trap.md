# Middle Innovation Trap

**Definition**: a state in which a country attains high **implementation capability** (strong at
production, process improvement) but fails to develop **concept design capability** (asking big
questions, designing market-leading concepts/products) → growth stalls again even after escaping
the [[middle-income-trap]].

## Within the Course

- The focus of Topic Area 2 on the essay list ([[essays-instructions]]): escaping the trap
  requires a national-level, long-run effort coordinating firms – government – institutions.
- The proposed escape route: [[creative-accumulation]] — accumulating trial-and-error
  experience, mission-oriented policy; case study: South Korea's industrial trajectory as a
  lesson for Vietnam.
