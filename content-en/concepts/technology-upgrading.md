# Technology Upgrading

**Definition**: the process by which an economy's technological capability is upgraded in
order to deepen and diversify industrial activity, thereby raising growth potential. Failure
to build technological capability → slower long-run growth.

## Three levels of analysis (per the essay instructions)

Technology upgrading is analyzed at 3 levels — **firm – sector – macro** — each with its own
challenges and uncertainties for Vietnam ([[essays-instructions]], Topic Area 1).
