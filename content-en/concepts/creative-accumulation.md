# Creative Accumulation

**Definition**: the process of building the capabilities needed for sustained technological
development and for rising to technological leadership, moving beyond pure catch-up growth.
It requires: asking bold questions, persistently accumulating trial-and-error experience, and
scaling up from ideas to viable outcomes.

## In the course

- Presented as the **way out of the [[middle-innovation-trap]]** ([[essays-instructions]],
  Topic Area 2).
- Theoretical foundation: innovation theory + evolutionary economics + industrial policy.
- Policy prescriptions: coordinated, mission-oriented, long-term strategies — applicable at any
  stage of development.
- Central case study: South Korea's industry & technology trajectory — is it a model for
  Vietnam?
