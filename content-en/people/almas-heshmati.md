# Almas Heshmati

The professor teaching **Development Issues and Business Cycle** (Class 32, UEH /
UEH-Vietnam Netherlands Programme). Email: almas.heshmati@gmail.com.

## Role in the course

- Teaches all 10 topics — see [[overview]].
- Directly supervises the essay: approves abstracts (28/6), supports data/modelling via email,
  grades essays, selects 12–15 for Springer publication — see [[essays-instructions]].
