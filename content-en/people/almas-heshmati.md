# Almas Heshmati

Professor in charge of **Development Issues and Business Cycle** (Class 32, UEH /
UEH-Vietnam Netherlands Programme). E-mail: almas.heshmati@gmail.com.

## Role in the course

- Teaches all 10 topics — see [[overview]].
- Directly supervises the essay: reviews abstracts (28/6), assists with data/modelling via
  email, grades essays, and selects 12–15 papers for Springer publication — see
  [[essays-instructions]].

## Research directions (inferred from the professor's own reading list in the syllabus)

- **Productivity & technological change**: Heshmati & Rashidghalam 2020 (TFP, technology
  shifters, J. Productivity Analysis); Lööf & Heshmati 2006 (innovation–performance).
- **Circular economy**: Su et al. 2013 (China); Heshmati 2017 (review); Heshmati &
  Rashidghalam 2021 (urban circular economy, Sweden).
- **Infrastructure & urbanization**: Heshmati & Rashidghalam 2020 (urban infrastructure,
  China).
- **Digitalization**: Kuosmanen, Pajarinen & Heshmati 2026 (digital adoption, Finland).

→ Strategic implication: topics 6, 8, and 9 feature the professor's own papers — these are
likely to be emphasized in lectures and on the exam; essays that align closely with these
directions are more likely to receive in-depth supervision.
