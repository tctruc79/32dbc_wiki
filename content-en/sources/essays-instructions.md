# Essays Topics and Instructions

## Summary

Detailed instructions from [[almas-heshmati]] on the essay assignment (50% of the course
grade): process, deadlines, required structure, and a list of suggested topic areas.

**Cross-checked (2026-07-20)**: the `.html` version and the official PDF
(`List of essays and instructions Class 32 MSc Course Development Issues.pdf`) have content
**identical** to this `.md` version (same 9 sections, same 2 topic areas, same ~30 suggested
articles) — no additional information, no separate page needed.

## Essay Requirements

- Groups of 1–2 people; topic of free choice (within or outside the suggested list, as long as
  it is relevant).
- **Length**: 3,500–4,500 words (single author) / 5,000–6,000 words (co-authored), including
  tables and references.
- **Mandatory 9-part structure**: Abstract → Background → Theoretical framework → Data →
  Model → Estimation and testing → Result and analysis → Conclusion and policy
  recommendations → References.
- Nature of the essay: a **modified/updated replication** of a key paper, using recent
  Vietnamese data. Will be checked for plagiarism and generative AI use.
- **Deadlines**: abstract (200–250 words) → 28/6; complete essay → 13/9 (send to
  almas.heshmati@gmail.com).
- The 12–15 best essays will be selected for publication in the **Springer Southeast Asian book
  series** (already 2 books published from the 2023 and 2025 essay cohorts).
- Data: recommended sources are Vietnam National Statistics (GSO), Google Scholar, and Science
  Direct.

## Suggested Topic Areas

Centered on the technology–development trap topic cluster:

1. **Technology Catch-up & Middle Income Trap** — [[technology-upgrading]],
   [[middle-income-trap]]: the three dimensions of technology upgrading (firm/sector/macro),
   leapfrogging, industrial policy within global value chains.
2. **Creative Accumulation** — [[creative-accumulation]] as an escape route from the
   [[middle-innovation-trap]]: moving from implementation capability to concept design
   capability, lessons from Korea for Vietnam.

Accompanied by ~30 suggested articles on R&D, technological capability, and innovation policy
in Vietnam (the list is in the original file; most do not yet have a corresponding PDF among
the raw sources).
