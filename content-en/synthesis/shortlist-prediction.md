# Predicting the K32 Final-Exam 20-Paper Shortlist

This page answers: among the 49 deep-ingested papers (LN1–LN8), which 20 are most likely to land
in the official shortlist Prof. Heshmati will send on **30/8** for the **06/9** written exam
([[ln0-course-intro]])? Synthesized from [[exam-prep]] (the 49-paper lookup table + 10 thematic
clusters), [[k31-final-exam]] (the previous cohort's real exam, same course/professor), and
[[overview]] (the 10-lecture outline, including LN9/LN10 whose materials the professor hasn't
sent yet but whose reading names already appear in the syllabus/slides).

⚠️ **This is a prediction, not the real shortlist.** The professor has not announced an official
shortlist. This page is meant to guide early exam prep, and MUST be revisited when: (a) the real
shortlist is announced on 30/8, or (b) LN9/LN10 are ingested (very likely to bump a few Tier B
picks out of the real shortlist — see section 5).

L64, L71` are near-lock candidates for the K32 shortlist.

## 5. Limitations & follow-up once LN9/LN10 arrive

Section 1 already shows LN9/LN10 **certainly** also contribute to the real shortlist (K31's Q3
and Q5 both fall in these two lectures). So the 20-paper list in section 2 (drawn entirely from
the 49 LN1–8 papers) will almost certainly get squeezed once LN9/LN10 are ingested — several Tier
B picks will likely drop out of the real shortlist to make room for the Q3/Q5-style papers (Tam et
al. 2024, Robinson & Thierfelder 2024) plus 1–2 more from those two lectures. Once LN9/LN10 are
ingested, return to this page: add a new Tier S entry for any paper matching a K31 question (once
the name is confirmed), then re-review which Tier B picks should be downgraded.
