## Where the Prediction Went Wrong — the Methodological Lesson

Comparing the Top-20 prediction in [[shortlist-prediction]] (2026-08-11 version) with the real
20:

- **Correct in the main column: 4/20 (20%)** — L14, L26, L34, L104.
- **Correct in the runner-up column (that page's section 4): 4 more** — L15, L25, L55, L85.
  Counting both columns: 8/20 (40%).
- **In neither column: 12** — L32, L45, L46, L52, L61, L64, L71, L73, L86, L93, L95, L107.
  Eleven of those were excluded solely because of the "the professor won't repeat K31" premise;
  L46 was simply not picked.

**Root cause — one single mistake, not many small ones**: the 2026-08-11 version
OVER-INTERPRETED the professor's in-class remark ("this year will have different papers") into
an absolute rule excluding all 20 K31 papers. In fact he replaced only 9/20. Had the 2026-08-08
version been kept (mapping the K31 shortlist straight across), the hit rate would have been
**11/20 (55%)** instead of 4/20. The warning in section 1c of [[shortlist-prediction]] (written
2026-08-15 after ingesting [[k30-final-exam]], pointing out that the reading list is more
stable than the premise suggested) turns out to have been RIGHT, but was never used to revise
section 2.

**General lesson**: an unwritten, in-passing remark from the person setting the exam should
only lower the confidence placed on a direct precedent — it should never be used to void that
precedent entirely. Documentary evidence (the real K31 shortlist, the real K30/K31 exams)
outranks inference from a single spoken sentence.

## Exam-Prep Implications — 9 Days Left

1. **Narrow the scope from 62 papers down to 20.** The remaining 42 serve only as
   context/counterpoint — no need to memorize their figures.
2. **Prioritize the four trunk papers that have already been asked**: L64, L95, L71, L85 (see
   section 3).
3. **Prepare comparison answers WITHIN each lecture's pair** — real exams favor this format,
   and now that each lecture has exactly two papers the pairings are explicit: L14↔L15
   (Vietnamese policy ↔ measuring Chinese growth quality), L25↔L26 (cross-sector governance ↔
   governance/FDI/informal economy), L32↔L34 (bank competition ↔ natural resources/CSR),
   L45↔L46 (in-place vulnerability ↔ labor migration), L52↔L55 (microfinance training ↔ fintech
   ecosystem), L61↔L64 (total factor productivity ↔ poverty/inequality), L71↔L73
   (infrastructure ↔ human capital), L85↔L86 (measuring the circular economy ↔ applying it),
   L93↔L95 (artificial intelligence ↔ digitalization barriers), L104↔L107 (Chinese exporters ↔
   Vietnamese imports/exports).
4. **Practice**: [[k32-mock-exam-6]] and [[k32-mock-exam-7]] are two sets written specifically
   for these official 20; the five earlier sets ([[k32-mock-exam-1]]…[[k32-mock-exam-5]])
   remain usable, but 19 of their 30 questions fall outside the shortlist.
5. **Exam rules** remain as in K30/K31: 2 compulsory questions + at least 2 of 4 elective
   questions, 12.5 points each — see the rules section of [[k31-final-exam]].
