# Course Overview — Development Issues and Business Cycle

The course map. Sources: [[syllabus-2026]], [[essays-instructions]].

## General information

- **Instructor**: [[almas-heshmati]] (UEH), almas.heshmati@gmail.com.
- **Description**: A survey of Development Issues & Business Cycle topics, balancing theory ↔
  empirics through research papers, with applications to the Vietnamese economy.
- **Base textbook**: Todaro & Smith (2015), *Economic Development*, 12th ed. (TS).
- **Grading**: Essay/Assignment **50%** + Written Exam **50%** — must score ≥50% on EACH part
  to pass.
