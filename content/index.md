## Thông tin chung - <span class="en">General information</span>

- **Giảng viên**: [[almas-heshmati]] (UEH), almas.heshmati@gmail.com.<br><span class="en">
  **Instructor**: [[almas-heshmati]] (UEH), almas.heshmati@gmail.com.</span>

  qua các paper nghiên cứu, với ứng dụng vào nền kinh tế Việt Nam.<br><span class="en">
  **Description**: A survey of Development Issues & Business Cycle topics, balancing theory ↔
  empirics through research papers, with applications to the Vietnamese economy.</span>

  class="en">**Base textbook**: Todaro & Smith (2015), *Economic Development*, 12th ed.

  class="en">**Grading**: Essay/Assignment **50%** + Written Exam **50%** — must score ≥50% on
  EACH part to pass.</span>

## 10 chủ đề lecture (Course Outline) - <span class="en">Lecture Topics (Course Outline)</span>

   ([[l11-patel-2021-unconditional-convergence]]), Nunn 2019 ([[l12-nunn-2019-rethinking-econ-dev]]),
   Spolaore & Wacziarg 2013 ([[l13-spolaore-2013-deep-roots]]), Sasges & Takahashi 2025
   ([[l14-sasges-2025-vietnam-policies]]), Yin et al. 2025 ([[l15-yin-2025-china-hqed]]).
2. **Governance, Institutions and Policy Making** — [[ln2-governance-institutions-policy-making]]
   — readings: Acemoglu, Johnson & Robinson 2001 ([[l21-acemoglu-2001-colonial-origins]], colonial
   origins), Mauro 1995 ([[l22-mauro-1995-corruption-growth]], corruption), Besley & Ghatak 2010
   ([[l23-besley-ghatak-2010-property-rights]], property rights), Alesina & Giuliano 2015
   ([[l24-alesina-giuliano-2015-culture-institutions]], culture), Bizikova 2025
   ([[l25-bizikova-2025-water-energy-food-nexus]]), Huynh & Tran 2025
   ([[l26-huynh-tran-2025-fdi-informal-economy]]).
3. **Financial Crisis and Pandemics** — [[ln3-financial-crisis-and-pandemics]] — readings:
   Baron, Verner & Xiong 2021 ([[l31-baron-2021-banking-crises-without-panics]]), Jiang, Levine

   ([[l34-cai-2023-natural-resources-financial-development-vietnam]]), Kakhkharov & Yarashov
   2025 ([[l35-kakhkharov-2025-banking-geopolitical-change]]), Minford 2025
   ([[l36-minford-2025-monetary-policy-lessons]]).
4. **Agriculture, Climate Change and Natural Disasters** — [[ln4-agriculture-climate-change-natural-disasters]]

   ([[l44-vo-tran-2022-rural-vulnerability-vietnam]]), Tran et al. 2022
