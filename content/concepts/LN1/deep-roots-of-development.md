# Deep Roots of Development

**Định nghĩa**: dòng literature tìm nguyên nhân **fundamental** (địa lý, lịch sử, văn hóa,
ancestry) của khác biệt phát triển, thay vì proximate determinants (vốn, lao động, công nghệ).
Paper khung: [[l13-spolaore-2013-deep-roots]].<br><span class="en">**Definition**: a strand of
literature that looks for **fundamental** causes (geography, history, culture, ancestry) of
development differences, rather than proximate determinants (capital, labor, technology).
Anchor paper: [[l13-spolaore-2013-deep-roots]].</span>

## Các khối bằng chứng (theo slides LN1)
<span class="en">## Blocks of evidence (per the LN1 slides)</span>

1. **Geography**: giải thích 44% biến thiên income (latitude mạnh nhất) nhưng chủ yếu qua
   kênh **gián tiếp** (Neolithic transmission — 71% biến thiên adoption of agriculture).<br><span
   class="en">**Geography**: explains 44% of the variation in income (latitude matters most)
   but mainly through an **indirect** channel (Neolithic transmission — 71% of the variation in
   the adoption of agriculture).</span>
2. **Institutions & reversal of fortune** (AJR 2001/2002, Nobel 2025): settler mortality →
   good/bad institutions; nhưng reversal biến mất khi thêm Europe hoặc adjust ancestry.<br><span
   class="en">**Institutions & reversal of fortune** (AJR 2001/2002, Nobel 2025): settler
   mortality → good/bad institutions; but the reversal disappears once Europe is added or
   ancestry is adjusted for.</span>
3. **Populations, not locations**: persistence là đặc tính của con người mang theo khi di cư
   (Putterman & Weil 2010, migration matrix; Easterly & Levine 2012 — European ancestry
   share).<br><span class="en">**Populations, not locations**: persistence is a trait people
   carry with them when they migrate (Putterman & Weil 2010, migration matrix; Easterly & Levine
   2012 — European ancestry share).</span>
4. **Genetic distance (FST)**: khoảng cách phả hệ giữa populations là barrier cho diffusion
   của công nghệ/thể chế (Spolaore & Wacziarg 2009).<br><span class="en">**Genetic distance
   (FST)**: genealogical distance between populations acts as a barrier to the diffusion of
   technology/institutions (Spolaore & Wacziarg 2009).</span>
5. **Cơ chế truyền**: 4 chiều (genetic, epigenetic, behavioural, symbolic) → biological /
   cultural / dual transmission (Jablonka & Lamb 2005).<br><span class="en">**Transmission
   mechanism**: 4 dimensions (genetic, epigenetic, behavioural, symbolic) → biological /
   cultural / dual transmission (Jablonka & Lamb 2005).</span>

## Thông điệp then chốt
<span class="en">## Key message</span>

Lịch sử persistent nhưng **không deterministic**; hiểu deep roots như **barriers to
diffusion** → chính sách vẫn có vai trò giảm barriers (Japan → East Asia; Hong Kong
demonstration effect, Romer 2009). Bỏ qua biến dài hạn → đánh giá sai hiệu quả chính sách.<br><span
class="en">History is persistent but **not deterministic**; understanding deep roots as
**barriers to diffusion** means policy still has a role in lowering those barriers (Japan → East
Asia; the Hong Kong demonstration effect, Romer 2009). Ignoring long-run variables leads to
mis-evaluating policy effectiveness.</span>
